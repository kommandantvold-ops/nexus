## Executive Summary
AI-driven screen of 10k+ candidates from Materials Project (MP) and Inorganic Crystal Structure Database (ICSD).  
**Criteria:**  
- Ionic conductivity (σ_RT >3 mS/cm or E_a <0.25 eV predicted)  
- Li-metal stability (ESW >2.5 V, Pittirati >0)
